Import contacts or a meeting invite

Meeting invite: in the invitation email, save the attached invite.ics to Files, then tap “Import file…”. You get a group named after the meeting, with every attendee and the organizer.

iPhone: Contacts → open a contact (or a list) → Share Contact → Save to Files. Then tap “Import file…” and pick it.

Android: Contacts app → Fix & manage → Export to file (.vcf). Or contacts.google.com → Export → Google CSV.

Only name, company, job title and notes are imported. Phone numbers, emails and addresses are ignored.

Everything stays on this phone, in the Even app. Who's Who has no account, no server and no internet access.
